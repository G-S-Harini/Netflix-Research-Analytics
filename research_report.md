# Netflix Release Year Analysis

## Research Question
Do movies tend to have later release years than TV shows?

## Dataset Summary
Valid records: 8807
Movies: 6131
TV Shows: 2676

## Results
Movie median release year: 2016.0
TV Show median release year: 2018.0
Median difference: -2.0
Mann–Whitney U statistic: 5820528.0
P-value: 1.0

## Conclusion
There was insufficient statistical evidence that movie release years tend to be later.

## Limitations
The Netflix catalogue may not represent all entertainment titles.
Release year is not the date a title was added to Netflix.
This observational analysis cannot establish causation.
